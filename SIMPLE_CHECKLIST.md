# ✅ Simple Deployment Checklist

## Your Mission: Make the website go live!

### **Step 1: Open the Settings Page**

Click this link (it will open in your browser):

**👉 https://github.com/Anika-007/healthcare-ux-dashboard/settings/pages**

---

### **Step 2: Find the Dropdown Menu**

On that page, look for:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Build and deployment                │
│                                     │
│ Source: [▼ Deploy from a branch]   │  ← This dropdown
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### **Step 3: Click the Dropdown**

Click on where it says "Deploy from a branch" (or "None")

---

### **Step 4: Select "GitHub Actions"**

A menu will appear. Click on:

```
✓ GitHub Actions  ← Click this one!
```

---

### **Step 5: Done! 🎉**

The page saves automatically. You'll see a message like:
"Your site is live at https://anika-007.github.io/healthcare-ux-dashboard/"

---

## ⏱️ Wait Time

Your website is now building. Wait **2-3 minutes**.

---

## 🌐 Your Website Link

After waiting, visit:

**https://anika-007.github.io/healthcare-ux-dashboard/**
